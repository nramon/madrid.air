# Change Log

## v2.0.0 (2026-11-23)

 * Fix the NO and NO2 magnitude codes, which were swapped. The values of these
   two magnitudes change.
 * Drop support for the old fixed-width format, which is no longer published.
 * Support the CSV and TXT formats currently published at datos.madrid.es.
 * Require R 3.5.0 or later.
 * Remove the tidyr and dplyr dependencies.
 * Files spanning several years are sorted by year.
 * Update the data download URLs.

## v1.0.0 (2016-12-15)

 Initial release.
